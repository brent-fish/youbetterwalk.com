Shop Local.

The big-box stores are fine. These keep your money in Central Ohio - homegrown shops where the staff know the products because they use them, covering everything from everyday food to gear, treats, and the odd critter.

Mutts & Co.

Locations around Columbus, including Graceland, German Village, and Grove City • Columbus-founded in 2007, the local leader in healthy dog and cat food, with self-serve dog wash and nutrition counseling

Fangs & Fur

1724 Northwest Blvd, Fifth by Northwest • Local, independent, and owner-operated, with the largest raw-diet selection in Columbus and a holistic bent - right in our backyard

Hollywood Feed

2830 Fishinger Rd, Upper Arlington, and stores all over town • An honorable mention with an asterisk: these were PetPeople, born right here in Columbus in the '90s, until Memphis-based Hollywood Feed bought the hometown chain in 2022. The ownership left town; the stores, the staff, and the healthy-food focus stayed.

License Your Dog.

Ohio law requires every dog over three months to carry a current county license, renewed annually. It costs less than a bag of good treats, funds the county shelter, and it's the fastest way home if your dog ever gets lost. Get or renew a Franklin County dog license →
