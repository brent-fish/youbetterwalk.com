Common questions.

How much does a dog walker cost in Columbus with You Better Walk?

Private walks start at $23 for 20 minutes and run $30 for 30 minutes, $36 for 45 minutes, and $45 for 60 minutes. Drop-in visits start at $26, and overnight stays are $75 per night.

How far ahead should I book?

New clients should plan on 3-5 days from first contact to first walk or visit, because care starts after a free meet and greet at your home. We reply within 24 hours or one business day.

Do you walk more than one dog at a time?

No. Every walk is private. In a multi-dog home, each dog walks individually, and a second dog's back-to-back walk is $5 off.

Do you sit for cats?

Yes. Drop-in visits and overnight stays cover dogs and cats. Cat care includes feeding, fresh water, litter, enrichment, and a visit report with a photo.

Are you insured?

Yes. You Better Walk is insured through Pet Care Insurance. Brent has also passed an enhanced background check, is trained in first aid and CPR, and is a registered member of the National Association of Professional Pet Sitters.

Which parts of Columbus do you serve?

Upper Arlington, Grandview Heights, and Marble Cliff are our core neighborhoods. We also serve Clintonville and Worthington, where a small travel fee applies and is waived for recurring weekly clients. Other areas may be possible on request.
