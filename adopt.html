Adopt Local

Meet your next best friend.

Every pet in our care is family, and a lot of the best family comes from a shelter. Our own Steve - Chief Inspiration Officer, The Real Boss, Columbus Humane Alum #52395242 - spent time waiting for someone to pick him. Somebody wonderful is waiting for you too.

A word from experience: adopting an adult dog or cat means adopting a personality you can already meet. The shelters below will tell you honestly who's shy, who's a couch potato, and who needs a yard. Trust them - matching pets to people is their whole job.

Every organization here is verified active as of September 2026. No affiliations - just neighbors we trust.

For Dog People.

Columbus HumaneSteve's Alma Mater

3015 Scioto Darby Executive Ct, Hilliard • Walk-ins Tuesday-Saturday, 12-6 • Full-service humane society with dogs, cats, and small animals

Franklin County Dog Shelter & Adoption Center

4340 Tamarack Blvd, Columbus • (614) 525-3647 • 60-100 dogs available at any time; the adoption fee includes license, vaccinations, spay/neuter, and microchip - one of the best deals in rescue

Columbus Dog Connection

2761 Johnstown Rd, Columbus • 25+ years of rescue work, plus low-cost spay/neuter and a monthly vaccine clinic

Humane Society of Delaware County

Delaware • By appointment - apply on their site first • Cares for 1,600+ animals a year north of the city

CHA Animal Shelter

3765 Corporate Dr, Columbus (Westerville area) • No-kill shelter for dogs and cats, run by Citizens for Humane Action since 1972

For Cat People.

Pets Without Parents
